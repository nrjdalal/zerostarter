A fresh scaffold runs every row of the first table, each with a page of its own. The starter stops where a product decision starts: anything that picks a vendor, payments, email, AI, background jobs or translations, is a fork's own choice, so none of it is wired. Read both tables literally.

## When you need this

Reach for this page when weighing the starter, before building something that may already ship, or before asking for something that may be planned or left to forks on purpose.

## Planned

Each has a next step and no date:

- Activity log retention and indexes; today it grows without bound.
- Deployment targets beyond Vercel and Docker.
- Feature-flag management over PostHog or another service.
- Organization creation rules: names and limits.
- Passkey sign-in and management, built close to done and parked, so it resumes from that work.
- Security headers and a content security policy by default, and a rate-limit store that survives a restart; today the counters live in process memory.

## Undecided

Raised and kept with no verdict, so nothing here is scheduled:

- An authenticated WebSocket pattern; the one shipped socket is public.
- Keyset pagination for the data tables; a batch loaded mid-scroll can skip or repeat a row.
- The rate limiter's reading of the client address where Bun owns the socket; a request without a readable address gets a fresh key each time.

## Common questions

**Where did passkeys go?**

Parked close to done, so sign-in ships with two OAuth providers and magic link rather than a third method half-finished.

**Does the starter have feature flags?**

The `features` export in `packages/config/src/site.ts` switches whole surfaces per fork and is not a flag service; PostHog's flags reach the browser once analytics is on. Managing them is planned.

**Is there an RSS feed?**

Not today. One was built and removed; shipping it, putting it behind a feature, or leaving it to forks is undecided.
