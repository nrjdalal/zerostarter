The first table is what the starter ships, each row linked to the page that covers it; a fresh scaffold leaves the allowlist and the waitlist off until you pick them at `init` or flip them in config ([Features](/docs/manage/features)). The starter stops where a product decision starts: anything that picks a vendor, payments, email, AI, background jobs or translations, is a fork's own choice, so none of it is wired, with no dependency, no env var and no stub.

## When you need this

Reach for this page when weighing the starter, before building something that may already ship, or before asking for something that may already be considered or left to forks on purpose.

## Shipped

| [SEO routes](/docs/manage/seo)                                             | `robots.ts` and `sitemap.ts` generate `robots.txt` and the sitemap.                                                      |
| [Waitlist](/docs/manage/waitlist)                                          | `/waitlist` on `POST /api/waitlist`, read at Console > Waitlist > Signups; optional, a fresh fork's home while on.       |

## Considered, not scheduled

Each is an idea with no date:

- Activity log retention and indexes; today it grows without bound.
- Deployment targets beyond Vercel and Docker.
- Feature-flag management over PostHog or another service.
- Organization creation rules: names and limits.
- Passkey sign-in and management; a near-complete build waits on the unmerged `feat/passkey` branch.
- Security headers and a content security policy by default, and a rate-limit store that survives a restart; today the counters live in process memory.

## Undecided

Raised and kept with no verdict, so nothing here is scheduled:

- An authenticated WebSocket pattern; the one shipped socket is public.
- An RSS feed for the blog: shipped by default, behind a feature, or left to forks.
- How the rate limiter reads the client address where Bun owns the socket; [Rate Limiting](/docs/manage/rate-limiting#how-a-request-is-keyed) states the gap.
- Keyset pagination for the data tables; a batch loaded mid-scroll can skip or repeat a row.

## Common questions

**Can users sign in with a passkey?**

No, passkeys are an idea above. Sign-in ships with GitHub and Google OAuth, and magic link works once its plugin is declared and an email sender is added (the comment above `magicLinkEnabled` in `packages/auth/src/index.ts` names the line).

**Does the starter have feature flags?**

The `features` export in `packages/config/src/site.ts` switches whole surfaces per fork and is not a flag service; PostHog's flags reach the browser once analytics is on. Managing them is an idea above, not scheduled.

**Is there an RSS feed?**

No, an RSS feed is undecided above.
